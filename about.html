Working principles.

The job

A cue sheet records where each piece of music plays, how long it runs and who should be credited.

Our starting point

Help supervisors and coordinators review the gap between a late picture edit and the last approved sheet.

The first evaluation

Compare a completed pair of edits with the team’s existing reconform, cue-sheet and manual review workflow.

Which late cut would you test?
