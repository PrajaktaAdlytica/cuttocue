Questions

Before you bring a cut.

What do you need for the evaluation?

Licensed picture references, the approved cue sheet, the editor’s music list and a recording and credit catalog the data owner authorizes for review.

What if dialogue masks the music?

The reviewer can leave the recording open and request an isolated reference. Episode 105 demonstrates that case.

Who signs off?

The authorized music reporting coordinator verifies timing and credits before release. A preview download creates a fictional record.

How will we compare the results?

Agree the scoring rules, then compare review minutes, confirmed discrepancies, false flags and missed changes with the existing workflow.

Which late cut would you test?

Bring the person who signs the cue sheet and a past revision worth reviewing.
